# Chatbot research review

Reviewed 8 November 2026. The original research report was recovered privately and reviewed; it is not published with this repository.

The methods discuss interviews, observation, convenience/purposive sampling and content analysis, while the appendix reports aggregate customer satisfaction ratings. The report alone does not provide the questionnaire instrument, participant-level responses, sampling/recruitment log, response-rate denominator, consent wording or reuse permissions. Its declaration also raises institutional copyright questions. No participant outcomes or satisfaction statistics are promoted as verified portfolio results.

The existing rule-based chatbot and synthetic CRM example should remain separate from the report's broad discussion of AI/NLP. Passing software tests demonstrate implemented behaviour, not customer satisfaction, retention gains or LLM capability. No causal improvement claim follows from aggregate ratings without a documented comparison design.

Before extending the human-subject research: recover the original instrument and consent/rights documentation; clarify which methods were actually executed; establish response provenance, missingness, scale coding and recruitment dates; obtain suitable permission for any deidentified reuse; then reproduce descriptive statistics from authorised records. Do not collect new participant data or publish responses merely to fill this gap. This review is complete, but the research extension remains blocked by those inputs and rights.
